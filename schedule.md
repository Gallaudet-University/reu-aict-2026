## A typical week

Outside of the milestone events above, weeks settle into a rhythm:

- **Mondays** — full-cohort research seminar; one student or faculty member presents work-in-progress.
- **Tuesdays / Thursdays** — open lab time; mentors check in 1:1 with their REU students.
- **Wednesdays** — professional development workshops (paper reading, research ethics, applying to graduate school).
- **Fridays** — methods workshops and cohort social activities.
