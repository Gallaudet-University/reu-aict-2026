schedule

Ten-week roadmap for Summer 2026 (May 19 – July 24).

The program runs ten weeks in residence at Gallaudet University. Each week has a research focus, and key shared events — orientation, mid-summer presentations, the final symposium — are listed under the weeks they fall in.

Move-in

Tuesday, May 19 — Arrivals

REU interns arrive in Washington, D.C., check into on-campus housing, and meet program staff. Travel is reimbursed; the program coordinator is on call all day for arrival logistics.

Week 1 · May 20 – May 22

Orientation & getting started

  • Wed, May 20 · 9 am – 12 pm — REU Orientation: program overview, introductions, communication access, expectations.
  • Thu, May 21 · 9 – 11 am — Literature Review workshop: how to find, read, and take notes on research papers.
  • Lab meet-and-greets with faculty mentors; first project conversations.
Week 2 · May 25 – May 29

Project planning

  • Mon, May 25 — Memorial Day holiday (no scheduled REU activities).
  • Mentors and students finalize project plans and research questions.
  • Methods workshops kick off (study design, research ethics, IRB if needed).
Week 3 · June 1 – June 5

First cohort presentations

  • Wed, June 3 · 9 – 11 am — 3rd-Week Presentations: each student gives a short talk on their project plan and literature review.
  • Pilot data collection begins for projects that need it.
Week 4 · June 8 – June 12

Core research, part I

  • Focused project work in mentor labs.
  • Professional development: applying to graduate school, abstract writing.
Week 5 · June 15 – June 19

Core research, part II

  • Fri, June 19 — Juneteenth holiday (no scheduled REU activities).
  • Mid-summer check-ins between students and mentors.
Week 6 · June 22 – June 26

Mid-summer presentations

  • Wed, June 24 · 9 – 11 am — 6th-Week Presentations: progress talks, feedback from the full cohort and faculty.
  • Recalibration: refine research questions and timelines based on feedback.
Week 7 · June 29 – July 3

Building & analysis

  • Main data collection / system development / experiments.
Week 8 · July 6 – July 10

Analysis & writing

  • Analysis pass on collected data.
  • Paper / poster outline due to mentors by end of week.
Week 9 · July 13 – July 17

Pre-final presentations

  • Wed, July 15 · 9 – 11 am — 9th-Week Presentations: dry run for the final symposium, with cohort feedback.
  • Final week of analysis and writing.
Week 10 · July 20 – July 24

Final symposium & farewells

  • Tue, July 21 · 9 – 11 am — Paper Presentations: final research talks, open to faculty and invited guests.
  • Wed, July 22 · 9 am – 12 pm — Lunch & Poster Session: public poster session with lunch.
  • Thu, July 23 · 9 – 11 am — Paper Submissions: final paper drafts submitted to mentors.
  • Fri, July 24 · 1 – 3 pm — REU Farewells: closing reception, exit interviews, departures.

A typical week

Outside the milestone events above, weeks settle into a rhythm: Monday full-cohort research seminar with one student or faculty member presenting work-in-progress; Tuesdays and Thursdays open lab time with 1:1 mentor check-ins; Wednesdays professional development workshops on paper reading, research ethics, and applying to graduate school; Fridays methods workshops and cohort social activities.
