## Graduate mentors

Each REU student is paired with a graduate student or postdoc in their lab
who provides day-to-day mentoring. The 2026 graduate mentor team will be
introduced here in spring 2026.

## Program staff

- **REU Coordinator** — handles logistics, housing, travel, stipends, and
  is the first stop for any non-research question during the summer.
- **Accessibility Coordinator** — arranges interpreters, CART, and other
  accommodations.

## Partners

The program collaborates with partner labs at the National Institute of
Standards and Technology (NIST), the National Institutes of Health (NIH),
and industry research groups for site visits and recruiting talks.
