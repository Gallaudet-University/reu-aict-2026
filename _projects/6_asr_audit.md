Speech recognition error rates are not the same for everyone. Accent, age, gender, and disability status all affect how well ASR systems work — and Deaf users, including those who speak as well as those who use voice carryover with a sign language interpreter, are rarely included in benchmark studies.

This project designs and runs a careful audit comparing ASR performance for hearing speakers and for deaf speakers with varied speech profiles, across several leading commercial and open-source systems.

**What students do.** Help design the study (what counts as a "fair" comparison?), recruit participants, run the audit pipeline, and write up the failure patterns you find.

**Skills you might build.** Audit study design, ASR APIs and tooling, statistical comparison of group performance, writing for both technical and policy audiences.
