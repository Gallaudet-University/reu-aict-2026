What sounds in your environment do you actually want to know about? Doorbells, sirens, a baby crying, your name spoken across a room — and what do you *not* want? This project builds on prior work on smartwatch and phone notifications for environmental sounds.

Two strands run in parallel: a systems strand that explores on-device sound recognition (no cloud), and a UX strand that asks deaf and hard-of-hearing users which sounds matter, when, and how they should be displayed.

**What students do.** Run formative interviews and surveys; train or fine-tune small audio models; prototype watch- and phone-based notification UIs.

**Skills you might build.** TensorFlow Lite / on-device ML, audio classification, mobile UI prototyping, formative user research.
