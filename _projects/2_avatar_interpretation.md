Signing avatars — animated 3D characters that produce ASL output — are increasingly proposed as a substitute for human interpreters in transit announcements, emergency alerts, and online video. But "legible to a hearing engineer" is not the same as "comprehensible to a Deaf viewer."

This project runs comparison studies: same content, delivered by a human interpreter, a motion-captured signer, and an AI-generated avatar. We measure both comprehension (did you understand?) and acceptability (would you actually use this?).

**What students do.** Help design study protocols (with the program's IRB), recruit and run participants, code video data, and analyze quantitative and qualitative results.

**Skills you might build.** Study design, working with human subjects, IRB protocols, qualitative coding, statistics.
