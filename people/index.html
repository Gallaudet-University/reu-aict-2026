Raja Kushalnagar

Raja Kushalnagar is Professor at Gallaudet University, Director of the AI, Accessibility and Sign Languages (AIASL) Center, and Co-Director of the REU AICT 2026 site.

His research focuses on accessible computing, captioning, multimedia accessibility, and the fairness of AI systems for deaf and hard-of-hearing users. He has led NSF-funded research and training projects on accessible information technology and on broadening participation of deaf students in computing graduate study.

He directs the program and is the first point of contact for prospective applicants with questions about projects, accessibility, or fit.

Co-Director

Christian Vogler
Professor, Gallaudet University

christian.vogler@gallaudet.edu

Christian Vogler

Christian Vogler is Professor at Gallaudet University and Director of the Technology Access Program (TAP). He serves as Co-Director of the REU AICT 2026 site.

His research focuses on accessible information and communication technology for deaf and hard-of-hearing people — including captioning, real-time communication access, sign language recognition, and the accessibility of emerging AI systems. He has led long-running NSF and federally funded research on these topics, with a particular emphasis on translating research into deployed, user-facing systems.

He shares responsibility for project matching, weekly cohort programming, and student mentoring throughout the summer.
