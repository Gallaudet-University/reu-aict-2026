## Eligibility

To apply, you must be:

- An undergraduate student at the time of the program (rising sophomores, juniors, and seniors). Graduating seniors are eligible if their degree is conferred *after* August 2026.
- A U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or permanent resident (NSF REU requirement).
- Enrolled in a degree program in computer science, information technology, human–computer interaction, linguistics, cognitive science, psychology, or a related field.

You **do not** need:

- Prior research experience
- A specific GPA (we read full applications holistically)
- Fluency in ASL — but we want students who are excited to communicate across deaf/hearing differences. ASL instruction is available during the program.

We particularly encourage applications from deaf, hard-of-hearing, DeafBlind, and DeafDisabled students; students at institutions that do not offer undergraduate research opportunities; and students from groups historically underrepresented in computing.

## What you submit

1. **Online application form** — basic info, school, year, intended major.
2. **Statement of interest (≤ 500 words)** — Why this program? What questions interest you? Which projects from our [Projects page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) would you most like to work on, and why?
3. **Resume or CV** — one page is fine.
4. **Unofficial transcript** — PDF.
5. **One letter of recommendation** — from a faculty member, instructor, or research mentor who can speak to your readiness for research. They submit it directly through a link we send them.

## How we read applications

We are looking for *potential and fit*, not polish. We read for:

- Curiosity and clarity about what you want to learn.
- A reasonable match between your interests and at least one of our projects.
- Evidence that you can stick with hard problems — that can come from coursework, jobs, hobbies, or community work, not just research.

We read every application in full. Decisions are made by a faculty committee.

## Costs covered

- $7,000 summer stipend
- Round-trip travel to Washington, D.C.
- On-campus housing
- A meal allowance for the duration of the program
- Conference travel support during the following academic year, if you present your REU work
