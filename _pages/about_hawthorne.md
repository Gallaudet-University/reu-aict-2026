## Kara Hawthorne

Kara Hawthorne is a faculty member at Gallaudet University and a 2026 REU AICT Faculty Mentor. Her research focuses on language and communication — bringing methods from linguistics and the language sciences to questions about how deaf and hard-of-hearing people acquire, process, and use language, and how technology can support that.
