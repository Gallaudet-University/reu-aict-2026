## Christian Vogler

Christian Vogler is Professor at Gallaudet University and Director of the Technology Access Program (TAP). He serves as Co-Director of the REU AICT 2026 site.

His research focuses on accessible information and communication technology for deaf and hard-of-hearing people — including captioning, real-time communication access, sign language recognition, and the accessibility of emerging AI systems. He has led long-running NSF and federally funded research on these topics, with a particular emphasis on translating research into deployed, user-facing systems.

He shares responsibility for project matching, weekly cohort programming, and student mentoring throughout the summer.
