## Chizuko Tamaki

Chizuko Tamaki is a faculty member at Gallaudet University and a 2026 REU AICT Faculty Mentor. Her work centers on audiology and hearing technology — including hearing assistive technology, listening in real-world environments, and the design and evaluation of devices and services that improve access for people with diverse hearing profiles.
