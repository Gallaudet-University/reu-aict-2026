### A typical week

Outside the milestone events above, weeks settle into a rhythm: **Monday** full-cohort research seminar with one student or faculty member presenting work-in-progress; **Tuesdays and Thursdays** open lab time with 1:1 mentor check-ins; **Wednesdays** professional development workshops on paper reading, research ethics, and applying to graduate school; **Fridays** methods workshops and cohort social activities.
