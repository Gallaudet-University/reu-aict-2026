## Raja Kushalnagar

Raja Kushalnagar is Professor at Gallaudet University, Director of the AI, Accessibility and Sign Languages (AIASL) Center, and Co-Director of the REU AICT 2026 site.

His research focuses on accessible computing, captioning, multimedia accessibility, and the fairness of AI systems for deaf and hard-of-hearing users. He has led NSF-funded research and training projects on accessible information technology and on broadening participation of deaf students in computing graduate study.

He directs the program and is the first point of contact for prospective applicants with questions about projects, accessibility, or fit.
