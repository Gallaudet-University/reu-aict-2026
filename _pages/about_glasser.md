## Abraham Glasser

Abraham Glasser is a faculty member at Gallaudet University and a 2026 REU AICT Faculty Mentor. His research sits at the intersection of accessible computing, sign language technology, and human–computer interaction — including computer vision and machine learning for ASL, captioning and speech access, and the design of technology with and for the Deaf community.
