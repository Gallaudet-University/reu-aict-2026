The **REU AICT** program is a 10-week paid summer research experience at Gallaudet University — the world's only university for deaf and hard-of-hearing students — where undergraduates do real research at the intersection of **AI, accessibility, and human-computer communication**.

Each summer, 10 undergraduate researchers join faculty-led projects spanning sign language and multimodal AI, captioning and speech access, accessible HCI, and the fairness of modern AI systems for Deaf communities. The program runs in **American Sign Language and English**, and is designed so deaf and hearing students collaborate as peers from day one.

### Summer 2026 at a glance

- **Dates** — May 19 – July 24, 2026 (10 weeks)
- **Stipend** — $7,000 for the summer
- **Housing** — on-campus housing provided
- **Travel** — round-trip to Washington, D.C. covered
- **Cohort** — 10 undergraduate researchers
